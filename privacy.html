Snugrid: Block Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Snugrid: Block Puzzle (“Snugrid”, “the app”) is a casual block-puzzle game for iOS and Android, published by Florian Moscu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. You do not need an account to play.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, settings and statistics are stored only on your device (in the app’s local storage). We do not receive them and we have no user accounts or servers for this data. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use data such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before showing personalised ads we ask for your consent through Google’s consent message (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → AD PRIVACY.
  • iOS: if the app asks to track you across other companies’ apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), you can say Ask App Not to Track and still play; ads then are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Optional purchase (removing ads)

If the app offers an in-app purchase to remove ads, the payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details; we only learn that the purchase was made, so that ads stay off.

4. Analytics and crash data

The current version does not send analytics or crash reports to us. If a later version does, this policy will be updated first.

5. Children

Snugrid is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google through ads, you can use Google’s tools (myadcenter.google.com). If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you also have the right to object, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
